    The rules this journey follows

    Decided with Keith (YouMind), 17 Nov 2026.

    • Nobody sees the resultsNot NPI, not coaches, colleges or recruiters, and not parents. Parents give consent and pay; they get those emails and a receipt, nothing more.
    • Free first, then one clear priceOne free voice check-in and a starter insight per athlete, within the first 90 days. Then USD 49, once, for the full profile and a 90-day protocol. Nothing renews; under 18, the parent pays.
    • Age decides the pathUnder 13 can’t take part. 13 to 17 need a parent’s consent by email before the check-in. 18 and over consent for themselves.
    • Every sign-up is counted as NPIEach QR code carries its own tag (?src=npi&from=flyer, card or guide). The first source is kept for 90 days and never overwritten; typing the code NPI also counts. The real build saves it with the sign-up and sends it to the CRM as lead source NPI.
